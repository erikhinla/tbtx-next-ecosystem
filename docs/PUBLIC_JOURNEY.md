# Public journey contract

The site continues the AI/human essay. The scan and the map are the first stand. Products are how the stand becomes a day you can live.

This is the public sequence. Internal product language (WIN, GOAL, FLOW, Quad Keystones, BizBot) stays off the cold path.

## Sequence

1. **Social post** — AI is changing every aspect of life and work. The trap is not only replacement. The trap is becoming the coordinator of leftovers.
2. **Arrival** — The baked film is the greeting. Do not write a second hero on top of it. Hero `Start Here` goes to `#tbtx-arrival`, never to a door.
3. **Stand** (`#tbtx-stand`) — Speak to one person. Mirror the leftover job. No quiz talk, no runtime, no "pick a door."
4. **Choose Your Path** (`#tbtx-stakes`) — Three named positions, meaning on the face.
   - Sit out: ignore the infrastructure. Get outrun. Footer: `Choose this consequence`. Site stays closed.
   - Sit back: agents start the creative. You coordinate the context. Footer: `Choose this consequence`. Site stays closed.
   - Stand UP: get clarity and momentum in work and life. Only this path opens the rest of the site.
5. **Doors** (`#tbtx-doors`) — Only after Stand UP. Enter signs, same weight.
   - `Enter to Scan for Digital Fog in Life` → `/tbtx/scan`
   - `Enter to Map Digital Fog in Business` → `/tbtx/map`
6. **Mosaic** — Lived leftover jobs. Last tile is `What's yours?` Submissions are scenes: name, what they were trying to finish, what they actually did.
7. **Threshold** — `/tbtx/scan` and `/tbtx/map` restate the stand before question 1. Deep links from social get this even if they skipped the front door. Begin labels: `Begin the Scan` / `Begin the Map`.
8. **Name it** — Above Q1: `Find the leftover job.`
9. **Follow-through** — Result is a receipt, not a new pitch. Kit, blueprint, and backbone are instruments.
10. **Close** — The refrain, then `Choose a door` back to the fork.

## Refrain

Same sentence, unchanged, three places:

> You don't need more AI. Clear the fog.

Stand path. Scan and map threshold. Close. Not over the hero film.

## Rule

Every surface answers: does this still serve the stand, or did we switch into selling a diagnostic?

`Start Here` is the entrance to Arrival. It is not a shortcut to a door, a quiz, or a product.

Do not be clever at the expense of knowing what to do. Choice meaning lives on the face, before the click.
