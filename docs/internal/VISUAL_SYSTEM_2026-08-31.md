## Typography

| Family | Job |
|---|---|
| Outfit | Live editorial display and body system |
| Archivo Black | Macro campaign statements and forceful lockups |
| DM Sans | Supporting copy where a quieter reading voice is needed |
| JetBrains Mono | Labels, routes, counts, diagnostics, technical annotations |

Display type is oversized, tightly tracked, left aligned, and allowed to break the grid. Supporting type stays narrow enough to read in one pass. Small labels use generous tracking and never compete with the headline.

## Motion grammar

1. Fog reveals meaning; it is not decoration.
2. Slow image scale creates environmental motion when film is unnecessary.
3. Type may blur, dissolve, or emerge as the viewer moves through the story.
4. Route lines move from fragmentation toward a visible destination.
5. Hover motion reveals evidence or a next action; it never performs without purpose.
6. Use transform and opacity. Respect reduced-motion settings.
7. No bouncing interface motion, neon glows, or generic technology loops.

## Composition grammar

- Full-bleed media for recognition and consequence.
- Asymmetrical editorial grids for explanation.
- Sticky narrative beside moving evidence for deeper chapters.
- Large quiet fields before a decisive statement.
- Hairlines, route marks, and small labels instead of boxed feature cards.
- Images carry atmosphere; text carries the argument.
- One strong action per chapter.
- Mobile becomes a deliberate vertical sequence, not a compressed desktop grid.

## Asset standard

Use an asset when it does at least one of these jobs:

- creates recognition
- visualizes the hidden coordination job
- distinguishes fog from friction
- makes infrastructure visible
- demonstrates governed execution
- clarifies the next route

Reject or crop assets with fake browser chrome, conflicting navigation, unreadable generated type, invented claims, duplicated copy, weak focal hierarchy, or generic AI imagery.

Assets older than seven days move to reference status until they are reviewed against the current live system.

## Production gate

Before a public asset ships:

- route and audience are named
- message is source-supported
- lane is personal, business, category, or approved
- desktop and mobile crops are checked
- embedded type is readable and accurate
- visual language matches the destination page
- motion has a narrative job
- CTA lands on the correct customer step
- human review approves the result
