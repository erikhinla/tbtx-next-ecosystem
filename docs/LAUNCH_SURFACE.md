# Digital Fog launch surface

Live route: `https://transformby10x.ai/tbtx/hub`

This route is the internal campaign workspace. It does not replace the public story at `/tbtx`.

## Customer and revenue flow

1. Attention: campaign hook and hero film.
2. Recognition: stakes and lived leftover-job scenes.
3. Personal: `/scan` to Fog Check, then `/tbtx/kit` to Fog-Free Daily at $7.77.
4. Business: `/map` to Momentum Map, then `/bbai` to BizBuilders AI.
5. Later-stage automation stays after business readiness.

The bridge between personal and business is: `Same fog. Different lives.`

## Launch utility

- Route links can be copied from the Route section.
- The Convert section maps each customer step to its public surface and revenue destination.
- Selects are labeled by lane: green approved, purple life, blue business, gray category.
- Ship, Hold, and Skip choices persist in the current browser.
- Stills can be downloaded from their cards.
- Approved campaign lines can be copied from the Say section.
