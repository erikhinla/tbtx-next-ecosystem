01 / The spine

The world built the generator.
It didn't build the spine.

AI made more work, not less. The output arrives and the job still isn't finished. Someone has to move the context, check the answer, find the version, and hand it on.

We named that job Digital Fog. TransformBy10X is what we do about it.

We find the fog. We build somewhere for the fix to live. We keep the work moving.

Twenty years of marketing operations and project management taught us where handoffs break. The last three years we've been building with AI on the crew. Not a demo. A colleague.

What's missing isn't more output. It's the spine. We build it. Then the momentum is yours.
