01 / The connection

The world is missing a bridge.

The output arrived. The job didn't finish. That's the leftover job. We build the structure underneath it.

AI made more work, not less. Someone still has to move the context, check the answer, find the version, and hand it on. The world built the generator. It didn't build the spine.

We named that work TransformBy10X. We find the fog. We give it a home. We keep it moving.

Twenty years of marketing operations and project management taught us the handoffs. The last three years, we've been building with AI on the crew. Not a scene. A colleague.

What's missing isn't more output. It's the structure underneath. We help bridge the gap. Then we gain momentum.
