# The work between the work

## Founder introduction

I’m Erik. I’ve spent more than 20 years in marketing operations and project management, making sure work shipped.

For the past three years, I’ve been learning and applying AI tools and systems. I’ve spent the past year building TransformBy10X, BizBuilders AI and BizBot Mrktng.

The connection is practical. I’ve worked with the people, the deadlines and the handoffs. Now I’m building the structure underneath them: context that stays, decisions people can find, and a clear next action.

I want more room for the work people care about. That’s what I’m trying to build.
