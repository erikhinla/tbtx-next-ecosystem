# COPY RULES (TBTX, BBAI, BBM, erikhbush.com)

Read before any copy task. On every draft, first run the `humanizer` skill (strip AI-isms; never alter approved Copy Bank lines; no em dashes or emojis), then run `python3 /root/tbtx-next-ecosystem/hermes/copy_check.py <draft.txt>` before replying. Start creative tasks in a fresh session (/new).

## Voice
- No em dashes.
- No reader-directed or possessive "you", "your", "you're", "yourself". Write neutral, system-level lines people recognize themselves in.
- Use contractions (it's, isn't).
- Hooks blame the system, never the reader.
- No question marks in hooks or headlines (no rhetorical questions).
- No vague promises or puffery ("better way", "the secret", "game-changer", "unlock", "seamless").

## Banned words and phrases
TRACE, tracing, leak, bleed, breaks down, completion review, walk with me, readout.

## Claims and naming
- Name no tools or vendors.
- No unbacked stats.
- No trademark symbols (™ ® ℠ ©).
- Never say AI was hired or that people work for AI. "People are assisting AI" is allowed.
- "Context Coordinator" is never a job title.
- PROOF never names departments.

## Personal (erikhbush.com)
- Separate concepts only: circumstances, and industry/category commentary.
- Never adapted TBTX copy. Never mentions PROOF.

## Logistics
- TBTX X handle: @Transformby10x
- Posting times in Pacific time.

## Instructions
- Never invent instructions the user didn't give. Quote only what the user actually said.

## Business terms
- Say "Digital Friction" (the business term, tied to the Friction Audit). Do not use "Digital Fog" in TBTX business copy.
- Never write that fog "hardens into" friction.
- Every line must say what it refers to: no vague "It's..." openers, and no "Context that holds" without saying what it holds.

## Visual copy (from DESIGN-GUIDE.md v4)
- URL goes in the caption only. Never put a URL, domain or "visit" line on an image or video frame. On Instagram the caption says "link in bio".
- Brand colors are accents only (about 10% of a frame). Backgrounds are neutral (#111111 or #fafaf7).
